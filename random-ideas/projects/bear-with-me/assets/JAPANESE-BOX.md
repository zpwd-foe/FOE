# Japanese box material and construction

The animated box follows the approved [v7 preview](../../../previews/japanese-box-v7.png). Its original perspective and moving lid geometry are retained. The asanoha inlay, six matching corner fingers and dark iron fittings are native SVG geometry, independent of the material texture. The toggle mounts directly into the continuous ledge. Its geometry is shared by the switch drawing and paw contact targets; the button follows the rendered fitting's actual bounds on resize and movement. The green indicator is inset from the front edge.

`hinoki-grain-v1.png` is a 1536 × 1024 material image, generated with built-in ImageGen from v7 and copied unchanged into this directory. Surface projection and lighting are applied by the renderer. The preview itself is not flattened into the animation.

## Texture prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: flat diffuse wood texture for the moving SVG surfaces of a Japanese wooden box.
Reference image is the approved box design. Extract its MATERIAL APPEARANCE into a new plain rectangular, edge-to-edge wood texture: pale honey hinoki cypress, matching the light front ledge and the lid in the reference. Delicate continuous fine parallel horizontal grain with a few subtle natural waves, pale cream growth lines and muted honey-tan fibres. Restrained saturation, natural satin surface, unvarnished-looking fine pores. The texture represents one clear-grained board, evenly lit and photographed perfectly straight on. Entire image filled with wood, no edges or borders.
Do not render a box or bear or any object. NO pattern, asanoha, inlay, joints, hardware, plaque, knots, seams, dark orange stain, red mahogany, hard shadows, highlights, gradients or perspective. This is a neutral albedo material map; surface lighting will be supplied by the animation. Landscape 1536 by 1024 pixels. Preserve the reference's LIGHT HONEY color and fine realistic grain.
