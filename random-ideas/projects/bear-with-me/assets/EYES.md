The surrounding cheek stays golden, and the muzzle's foreground mask keeps the cream snout unchanged.

Three uses of one animated mouth path provide its soft shadow, brown stitching and fine highlight, so every expression keeps the same relief. The chin casts a soft, tapered shadow onto the visible upper belly; its position responds to head tilt. Paw shadows follow the hands across the torso. A broad belly gradient adds roundness, while the darker opening shades the lower belly at a fixed box height as the bear rises or retreats. All body shading is clipped to the original plush alpha, preserving the furry silhouette. These are SVG lighting layers over the existing artwork; no raster asset changes were needed.

The shoulder roots sit below the neck, with their fur tucked behind the torso. Each arm is one continuous curved fur surface from shoulder to wrist, rather than two rotating sprites with a separate elbow cap. A fixed outward/downward elbow bias maintains the fold when the paw crosses the shoulder. The surface straightens as reach increases and compresses on the inside of a bend. An opaque inset fabric lining seals semitransparent source pixels and antialiased triangle seams; the outer fur fringe retains its soft alpha. The arm, paw and held props change depth together across the box rim and retract together, keeping the exposed upper arm visible during a reach.
