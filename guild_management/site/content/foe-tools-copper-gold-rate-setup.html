The settings to remember

Two tiers, two contribution rates

Open FOE Tools GB Investment (opens in a new tab), select your GB, and enter the bonus values below.

Copper tier
1.90×Arc bonus (%) 90
Gold tier
1.10×Gold contribution bonus (%) 10

Enter the values in percentage mode (%). The Gold field sets the contribution bonus for Gold levels; the two percentages are separate settings.

Important: Switching GBs may reset Gold contribution bonus (%) to 0. Check that it is 10 before priming and again before posting.

Set up the calculator

This walkthrough uses Tower of Babel, going from level 17 to level 18. The green numbers in each screenshot match the numbered steps.

  1. Select Owner view. Use this view when preparing and priming your own GB.
  2. Select Compact. This shows inputs, results, and message selection together in one table.
  3. Select your GB. The example uses Tower of Babel.
  4. Enter the target level. Enter the level your GB is going to. For a GB currently at level 17, enter 18.
  5. Set the Copper rate. Enter 90 in Arc bonus (%) for a 1.90 contribution rate.
  6. Set the Gold rate. Enter 10 in Gold contribution bonus (%) for a 1.10 contribution rate on Gold tier GBs.

Important: FOE Tools automatically chooses which rate to use based on your selected GB and target level. It uses the bonus values you enter.

Prime your GB

Priming means adding your own Forge Points (FP) to secure the reward positions you want to offer.

  1. Select Leave the last place open for a double dip. Use this setting when calculating your priming amount. Otherwise, the final contribution may level up (“flip”) your GB.
  2. Read Owner FP to secure. This shows the total FP you must have contributed to secure each position. Any FP you have already added counts toward this total. In this example, a total of 206 owner FP secures both P1 and P2.

Add the priming FP and confirm secured positions

Add any FP still needed to reach the required total to your GB in-game. Then, in FOE Tools, click the + beside the priming amount to update the calculator. In this example, the total is 206 FP.

P1 and P2 should turn green and show Secured. Only advertise positions once they are secured.

Set up the promotion message

  1. Open Message settings. Click the arrow beside the heading, then select Only secured positions and Prefix.
  2. Add your player name. Enter your player name under Prefix so others know whose GB they are contributing to.
  3. Check and copy the message. Confirm the listed positions and FP amounts, then click Copy message. Paste the message into the correct FoE & Friends thread for your GB’s tier.

Example promotion message

zpwd's ToB 2 (101) 1 (201)

This advertises P2 for 101 FP and P1 for 201 FP. Use your own player name and the current amounts from the calculator.

Before you post

  • The selected GB and target level match your GB in-game.
  • Arc bonus (%) is 90 and Gold contribution bonus (%) is 10. Recheck the Gold value after switching GBs; it may reset to 0.
  • Your owner FP and any existing investors match the current in-game contributions.
  • Every advertised position is secured, and the message includes your player name.

Before sending, check the positions and FP amounts. Post in the matching thread:
