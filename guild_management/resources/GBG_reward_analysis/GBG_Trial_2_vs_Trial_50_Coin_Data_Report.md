# Forge of Empires GBG Coin Reward Report

## Dataset Overview

Eight days of observed Guild Battlegrounds coin rewards are recorded
here.

-   **Days 1--4:** Trial 2
-   **Days 5--8:** Trial 50
-   Days 1--8 correspond to **10--17 September 2026**, respectively.
-   Daily fight totals are taken from the accompanying activity
    screenshot; the first screenshot row is Day 1. Day 4 and Day 5
    totals include the supplied corrections to 1,326 and 528 fights.
-   Silver, Gold, and Platinum are kept as separate currencies.
-   In each source record, the number before the coin name is the
    **coins awarded per drop**; the number after the colon is the
    **number of drops**.
-   Percentages are the observed share of drops on that day.

## Observed Reward-Mix Differences

The eight-day sample shows a very large shift in reward composition
between the two trials.

**Trial 2** - Silver dominates at approximately **82.8%** of observed
drops. - Gold accounts for approximately **10.8%**. - Platinum accounts
for approximately **6.5%**.

**Trial 50** - Silver falls to approximately **20.2%**. - Gold rises to
approximately **40.1%**. - Platinum rises to approximately **39.7%**.

Trial 50 also pays substantially more currency per drop: - Silver: **448
vs. 75**, about **5.97×** - Gold: **108 vs. 51**, about **2.12×** -
Platinum: **72 vs. 21**, about **3.43×**

The three currencies have different uses and should not be ranked by
amount alone. Silver is the only coin that can be exchanged for an
unlimited amount of Diamonds. The data quantify output and efficiency,
but whether either reward profile is worth its costs is left to the
reader.

Raw daily totals should not be treated as a direct measure of trial
efficiency because fight volume differs substantially between days and
between trials. Normalizing by observed fights, Trial 2 produced **49.57
reward drops per 100 fights**, while Trial 50 produced **66.62 reward
drops per 100 fights**. This is an observed association in this
eight-day sample, not proof that the trial setting alone caused the
difference.

## Suggested Next Analysis

With the raw data preserved, useful next steps include:

1.  Estimate the underlying Silver/Gold/Platinum drop probabilities for
    each Trial.
2.  Calculate confidence intervals around those observed probabilities.
3.  Compare expected currency yield **per reward drop** rather than
    simply per day.
4.  Measure day-to-day variance and test whether the observed reward
    distributions are consistent across days.
5.  Estimate the number of drops needed to generate target quantities of
    Silver, Gold, or Platinum.
6.  Extend the fight-normalized comparison to currency earned per fight
    and add attrition or other effort measures if they become available.
7.  Keep Silver, Gold, and Platinum separate unless an explicit economic
    conversion or utility model is defined.
