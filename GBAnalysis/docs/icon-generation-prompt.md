# Dashboard icon

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then resized into the optimized header and favicon assets. The original exploratory artwork was left unchanged and is not part of the production bundle.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand

Asset type: 56px website header icon and 32px favicon

Primary request: Create one elegant East Asian-inspired zen icon for the Hydrangea Tide Great Building dashboard.

Subject: A single incomplete ensō circle made from one graceful, gently tapered brushstroke. The circle opens at the upper-right. Its lower curve subtly broadens like a quiet ocean tide.

Style: Minimal, refined, feminine through proportion and flow; contemporary Japanese ink-mark restraint; clean enough to read instantly at 32px.

Composition: Centered, optically balanced, generous negative space. The mark fills about 70% of a square canvas.

Color palette: Full-bleed very pale mist-aqua background `#E1F1F4`. The single stroke transitions smoothly and subtly from muted aqua teal `#367985` at the lower-left through cool hydrangea blue `#6674B8` toward the upper-right. Keep contrast strong enough for a favicon, but quiet and sophisticated.

Constraints: Exactly one ensō brushstroke and nothing else. Square opaque raster image. Background reaches every edge; CSS will round the corners.

Avoid: letters, words, GB monogram, flowers, blossoms, leaves, buildings, architecture, scenery, mountains, sun, moon, water illustration, waves, extra symbols, border, ring around the canvas, white trim, shadow, bevel, 3D, neon cyan, saturated purple, gold, busy texture, mockup sheet.

## Color revision

Change only the color balance of the existing ensō brushstroke. Remove its green cast. Make the thick lower-left portion a cool soft blue-aqua, approximately `#4B879A`, then transition through desaturated hydrangea blue `#6879AE` into a slightly clearer periwinkle-blue `#7882BE` at the thin upper-right tip. Preserve the exact composition, texture, and pale mist-aqua background.

## Stroke revision

Make the brush character remain visible at 56px: strengthen the taper, make the lower sweep slightly bolder, add two or three broad dry-brush channels through the thick portion, and give the endpoints visibly feathered bristle tips. Use large, restrained marks rather than fine grain or noisy scratches.

## Background treatment

Remove the pale mist-aqua canvas during asset processing and export the final header icon and favicon with true transparency. This lets the ensō sit naturally on the dashboard background in both light and dark themes without a visible tile or trim.
