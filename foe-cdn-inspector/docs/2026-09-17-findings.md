# Initial findings: 2026-09-17 beta changes

This note records the first scan performed on September 18, 2026. The report ID is `2026-09-17_11-16-22`, and the `zz1.forgeofempires.com` bootstrap identified market `zz`, language `en`, and game URL `https://zz0.forgeofempires.com`.

## What changed

- 41 CDN files were added: all are PNG images. Most are reused item-store backgrounds and banner flaps across seasonal stores; two are new quest-marker HUD effects.
- 74 CDN assets were updated: 43 PNG images and 31 JSON files.
- All 31 asset JSON files are small texture-atlas descriptors with the same five top-level keys: `image`, `size`, `scale`, `pma`, and `frames`. They are machine-readable sprite metadata, not narrative text.
- The metadata diff references 22 newly added and 10 updated building entities. Their current public metadata JSON downloaded successfully.
- Nine English strings were added and two were removed.
- Six metadata families changed: `building_entity_lookup`, `building_upgrade_paths`, `building_upgrades`, `info_screens`, `limited_buildings`, and `selection_kits`.

The complete machine-readable record is in the generated `snapshots/2026-09-17_11-16-22/inventory.json` (ignored by Git by default).

## Building content

The 22 added building records consist of two new `HAL24` levels and twenty `HAL26` records:

- `Eerie Delirium Coaster` and `Ascended Eerie Delirium Coaster` extend the `HAL24` chain as A11/A12. Both occupy 4×7.
- `Lost Castle` is a 6×4, nine-level `HAL26` chain followed by the `Mystery Castle` and `Weeping Castle` variants.
- The other `HAL26` buildings are `Lycan Pavilion` / `Ascended Lycan Pavilion` (3×2), `Wraithrider Post` / its ascended variant (2×3), `Thornhollow Woods` (4×6), `Sootkettle Corner` / its ascended variant (3×3), and two levels of `Ectobrook Hollow` (4×3).

At Space Age: Space Hub, the top Lost Castle variants in this snapshot advertise:

- Mystery Castle: 300% all-attacking-army attack/defense, 300% Guild Expedition defending-army attack/defense, 250 Forge Points, and 240 current-age goods per motivated 24-hour collection, plus fragments.
- Weeping Castle: 400% all-attacking-army attack/defense, 450% Guild Expedition defending-army attack/defense, 300 Forge Points, and 280 current-age goods per motivated 24-hour collection, plus fragments.
- Thornhollow Woods: 400% all-attacking-army attack/defense, 400% city-defending-army attack/defense, 25% Guild Raids city-defense boost, 450 Forge Points, 400 current-age goods, and 350 previous-age goods per motivated 24-hour collection, plus fragments/random rewards.

These values are beta metadata and can change before release. The labels above are interpretations of internal boost identifiers; the raw records remain authoritative.

## New text signals

The new strings point to gameplay wording for:

- enhanced critical hit, including a 150% damage result and PvP Arena/Rogue exclusions;
- enhanced spoils of war with a limited number of eligible victorious main-city battles per collection;
- QI settlement double collection, now explicitly excluding the Town Hall.

One changed critical-hit string appears to be a wording clarification rather than an entirely new mechanic.

## Interpretation

The batch looks like a coordinated beta content update centered on Halloween 2026 buildings, upgrade/selection-kit wiring, and item-store presentation. The “text files” visible directly under `/assets/` are overwhelmingly texture metadata; the richer gameplay data is exposed through the content-hashed `/start/metadata?id=...` JSON endpoints referenced by the building lookup changes.
